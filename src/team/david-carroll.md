David joined M&E in 2007 and now leads the firm's Electrical Department as a principal and electrical project engineer. His work spans airports, schools, healthcare, military and municipal facilities, covering power and emergency power, lighting, fire alarm, and low-voltage and structured cabling systems. He is known for clear, buildable construction documents and for staying with a project through construction administration to make sure what gets built matches the design.
